\# Household Chores – My Project Plan



\## Goal



Build a simple, intuitive household chore manager for two people.



The application should be easy to understand even for people who are not interested in technology. It should feel practical and smart without becoming complicated.



\## Users



The application is designed for exactly two people.



\* Both people can choose their own names.

\* No login or user accounts are required.

\* A task can belong to Person A, Person B, or both.



\## Core Features



\### 1. Chore Management



Users can manage household chores in one simple place.



\* The app comes with around 8–12 common household chores, such as:



&#x20; \* Vacuuming

&#x20; \* Mopping

&#x20; \* Cooking

&#x20; \* Taking out the trash

&#x20; \* Cleaning the bathroom

&#x20; \* Laundry

&#x20; \* Tidying up

\* Users can add their own chores.

\* Chores can be edited or deleted.

\* Tasks can be one-time or recurring.

\* Recurring tasks can repeat daily, weekly, or monthly.

\* Tasks can be marked as either \*\*Normal\*\* or \*\*Important\*\*.



\### 2. Smart Assignment



Each chore can be assigned to:



\* Person A

\* Person B

\* Both people



Recurring chores can optionally rotate automatically between the two people.



Example:



\* Mopping → always Person A

\* Cooking → always Person B

\* Tidying → both

\* Cleaning the bathroom → rotates between A and B



If a task is assigned to both people, only one person needs to mark it as completed.



The application records who completed the task.



\### 3. Calendar and Task Tracking



The main interface is a simple calendar.



\* Weekly view is the default view.

\* Monthly view is also available.

\* Tasks clearly show whether they are open or completed.

\* Important tasks are visually distinguishable.

\* Due and overdue tasks are highlighted.



For overdue tasks, the app asks the user what to do:



\* Mark as completed

\* Move to today

\* Choose a new date



Optional in-app reminders can be enabled or disabled.



No email, SMS, or push notifications are required.



\### 4. Monthly Statistics



At the end of the month, the application shows a simple summary.



It includes:



\* Number of completed chores per person

\* Completion percentage

\* Total completed and open chores



For shared chores, the completed task counts for the person who actually marked it as completed.



The statistics should stay simple and easy to understand.



\## Interface



The application should have a minimal structure:



1\. Dashboard / weekly calendar

2\. Monthly calendar

3\. Add or edit chore

4\. Monthly statistics



The interface should avoid unnecessary menus and settings.



\## Out of Scope



To keep the homework manageable, version 1 will not include:



\* User accounts or authentication

\* Email notifications

\* SMS notifications

\* Push notifications

\* Mobile apps

\* Complex analytics

\* Points or gamification

\* Complex scheduling rules



The priority is a stable, useful, intuitive first version rather than a large feature set.
